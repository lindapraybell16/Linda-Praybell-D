About me

Issue #1

Hi, I'm Linda. I'm at the start of my story, and I like that.

I'm a first-year B.Tech IT student from Tamil Nadu. I picked IT because I liked the moment when something I typed actually showed up on the screen and did what I asked.

Most days I'm figuring things out: why a layout breaks, why a loop never ends, why my code works only after I add a semicolon. I used to find that frustrating. Now I treat each bug like a small mystery.

I like making things that feel like someone made them on purpose. This site is one of those things. Every border and every dot in it was set by hand.
